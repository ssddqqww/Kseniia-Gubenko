# Portfolio content notes

## Positioning

The strongest narrative is not "student who has done many activities." It is:

> A computer science student who builds technical systems at the intersection of AI, research, cybersecurity, education, and public impact.

The site prioritizes evidence over long lists: shipped products, original research, team leadership, measurable reach, and selective recognition.

## Primary proof points used

- Forgotten Scientists: founder, 10+ person team, 100+ profiles, 45K+ visits, 6.5K followers.
- Golden Minute: founder and product lead, eight-person team, 77 survey respondents, 30 MVP testers, Top 20 of 120 projects in She Leads 2.0.
- IOAI Ukraine: ranked 11th of 2,300 applicants after three stages.
- Junior Academy of Sciences: blockchain/UAV security prototype, third place in a 29-person cybersecurity section.
- Inclusive Academy: 75 tutoring hours, three neurodiverse students, later coordinated around 30 volunteers.
- Women's League NGO: supported 100+ volunteers and outreach to 800+ organizations and contacts.

- GPA appears as 11.9/12, 11.94/12, and 12/12. The site uses 11.94/12 from the Common App education section.
- Harvard Venture & Technology acceptance rate is described as estimated under 13%. The site avoids the estimated acceptance-rate claim.
- The PDF says IELTS 7.5, but the supplied CV and Common App text do not repeat it. The site omits IELTS until it is confirmed.
- The CV line containing "25,000 social media profile visits and ,000 content interactions" is incomplete. The interaction count is not used.
